# TRUE SURF — Web Research Dossier
Research date: 2026-09-28
Purpose: brand/product foundation for production Mini App and future landing.

## Executive summary

TRUE SURF already has a recognizable public character, but its public information is fragmented.

Verified recurring signals:
- Russian-speaking surf school serving Nha Trang / Bai Dai;
- beginner and progression lessons, individual and group formats;
- a strong personality anchor around Tony / @toni_t_ony;
- public positioning phrases: “Умный сёрфинг”, “Градация и система обучения”, “Комьюнити трушных серферов”;
- surf camp at Bai Dai;
- kitesurf trips to Phan Rang;
- friendly, social beach culture: music, fire, community, relaxed atmosphere;
- coaching language emphasizes a solid base, theory + practice, details from the first lessons and future progression.

Critical gaps in the public footprint:
- no reliably indexed official Team page listing the current four instructors;
- no reliably indexed official current price list;
- no public bios with instructor credentials/experience except the “10 years” positioning around @toni_t_ony;
- Instagram is referenced officially but its current feed is not reliably indexed/searchable;
- website truesurf.ru is publicly referenced but was not reliably fetchable during research.

## Pricing

### Source correction

The TRUE SURF pricing used in the VIIVERSION prototype did not come from invention or generic market research. It originated from TRUE SURF material supplied from the school's Telegram channel during product work in August 2026, including user-provided Telegram screenshots and subsequent price corrections.

Historical reconstruction:
- 2026-08-17: Telegram screenshots were used as client-provided pricing evidence. The prototype at that point recorded:
  - Group lesson — $50/person
  - Pair lesson — $60/person
  - Individual lesson — $70
  - Just Try — $100
  - Base Light — $150
  - Base Pro — $230
  - Skill Up — $240
- 2026-08-19: lesson pricing was updated in the working prototype to:
  - Personal 1:1 — $80
  - Personal 2:1 — $70/person
  - Personal 3:1 — $60/person
- By the first full Next.js rebuild committed on 2026-08-21, the source already contained the expanded price catalogue below.

Therefore, the earlier statement in this dossier that no official/current TRUE SURF price list had been found was methodologically incorrect: Telegram was the primary client source, but the public web index did not expose the channel price posts reliably.

## Key brand/product implications

1. Team must become visible.
The production Mini App should have a compact “school/team” layer because direct-entry users may never see the landing.

2. Tony/Anton is likely an important trust anchor.
If confirmed by client, his actual biography and 10+ year history should be a core proof point.

3. Anastasia’s role is potentially differentiated.
Photo/video + scheduling/conditions + instruction/customer care is a strong human-service signal, but her exact role and experience need confirmation.

4. The school’s unique strength is the combination:
system + progression + community + Bai Dai lifestyle.

5. Public pricing/team information is currently too fragmented.
The production project should centralize services, real prices, instructor profiles and actual availability.
